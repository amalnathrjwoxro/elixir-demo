Project Overview

Crafting Spaces Beyond Ordinary

Elixir Anfield is the fifth landmark project of Elixir Homes, offering premium flats for sale in Thrissur. Strategically located in Anchery, Kuttanellur — very close to HiLite Mall and the National Highway — this ongoing development is set amidst elite residential communities including Hill Gardens, Lesona Enclave, and Green Valley.

Prominent landmarks include Regency Club Kuttanellur and Chakolas Pavilion Anchery. Approved by State Bank of India, Canara Bank, LIC, Bank of Baroda and Bank of Maharashtra for seamless home loan processing. Completion and key handover expected by December 2027.
